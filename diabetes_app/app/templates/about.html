Limitations
  • The model was trained on an anonymised public dataset and has not been clinically validated.
  • HbA1c and blood glucose drive most of the prediction. Without them the model performs far worse.
  • Recall is {{ (meta.metrics.recall * 100)|round|int }}%, so some people who have diabetes will not be flagged. A low result does not rule out diabetes.
  • Inputs outside the training ranges are rejected because predictions there would be unreliable.
  • Results are a screening estimate only and never replace a doctor's diagnosis.
